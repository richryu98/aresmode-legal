This Privacy Policy describes how 아레스모드 (doing business as Aresmode) collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application Aresmode.

If you have questions, contact us at aresmode98@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Contact or authentication data (via Apple Sign In)
  • Health and fitness data (workout records, physical condition, body metrics)
  • Photos and videos you choose to submit for AI analysis (training or match footage, workout board photos)
  • Voice recordings you choose to make for voice logging

Sensitive Information

We process health data with your consent or as permitted by applicable law.

Payment Data

All payment data is handled by RevenueCat and Apple. We do not receive or store your card information. See their privacy policies: RevenueCat, Apple.

Health Data from Apple Health

With your permission, Aresmode reads health and workout data from Apple Health (for example heart rate, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, body mass, workouts, and active energy) to inform your training recommendations. Apple Health data is processed on your device and is never sold, and never used for advertising. You can revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Automatically Collected

  • Log and usage data (IP address, device info, app activity)
  • Device data (device model, OS version, app identifiers)

Device Permissions

Aresmode requests the following permissions. Each is optional and requested only when you use the related feature:

  • Microphone — voice workout logging
  • Speech Recognition — transcribing your voice logs
  • Camera — recording training or match footage for AI analysis
  • Photo Library (add) — saving share cards and reports you generate
  • Apple Health (read) — reading workout and recovery data (see above)
  • Apple Health (write) — writing workouts you log in Aresmode back to Apple Health

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver AI-powered workout prescriptions and analysis
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • To send administrative information (policy updates, etc.)
  • To protect our services and prevent fraud
  • To evaluate and improve our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only with the following third parties:

  • OpenAI — the AI models that generate workout prescriptions and analysis. Requests are routed through our infrastructure provider (Rork Toolkit / Vercel AI Gateway).
  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management
  • Apple — user account authentication and payment processing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Aresmode contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.

4. AI-Based Services

Aresmode uses AI models provided by OpenAI to generate personalized workout prescriptions and to analyze training or match footage you submit. The text of your training context (for example logged sessions, goals, and condition data) and any media you explicitly submit for analysis are sent for processing. Requests are routed through our infrastructure provider (Rork Toolkit / Vercel AI Gateway).

AI output is for training guidance only and is not medical advice. Aresmode does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe for any medical condition.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you have an account with us. Training records and analysis results are stored locally on your device. When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymize your data unless retention is required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Minors

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us at aresmode98@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccuracies in your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing

To exercise these rights, email us at aresmode98@gmail.com or delete your account from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account).

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the app or by email.
