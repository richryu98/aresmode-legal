This Privacy Policy describes how 아레스모드 (doing business as Aresmode) collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application Aresmode.

If you have questions, contact us at aresmode98@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Contact or authentication data (via Sign in with Apple on iOS or Google Sign-In on Android)
  • Health and fitness data (workout records, physical condition, body metrics)
  • Age and gender when you add them to your training profile
  • Photos or weekly-plan documents you choose to submit, and still keyframes extracted on your device from videos you choose for AI analysis
  • Voice-log transcripts created by the device speech-recognition service; live audio may be processed by Apple on iOS or the configured Android speech provider, while Aresmode does not retain the raw audio or send it to our AI providers

When you permanently delete an account, the Apple or Google identity proof sent to the deletion service can contain an email claim. The proof is used only to authorize that deletion request.

Sensitive Information

We process health data with your consent or as permitted by applicable law.

Payment Data

All payment data is handled by RevenueCat and the store where you obtained the app: Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. We do not receive or store your card information. See their privacy policies: RevenueCat, Apple, Google.

Health Data from Apple Health and Health Connect

With your permission, Aresmode reads selected health data. On iOS this can include heart-rate variability, resting and active heart rate, sleep, active and basal energy, workouts, body mass, exercise time, respiratory rate, one-minute heart-rate recovery, VO2 max, workout effort, and sleeping wrist temperature. On Android, Health Connect access can include exercise sessions, heart rate, distance, active calories, basal metabolic rate, RMSSD heart-rate variability, sleep, resting heart rate, weight, respiratory rate, VO2 max, and skin temperature. If you separately grant health-history access, Android can read up to 180 days instead of the normal 30-day window. Raw samples are processed on your device; when you ask for a personalized session, selected recovery and training values may be included in that AI request. Health data is never sold and never used for advertising. You can revoke access in iOS Settings or Health Connect.

Health Data Aresmode Writes Back

On iOS, Aresmode can also write to Apple Health, with your separate permission. It writes only the workouts you complete in the app, so that your training counts toward your activity rings and Apple's training load. Three things are written: the workout itself (type, start and end time), active energy only when a device actually measured it, and a workout effort score only when you rated the session's intensity yourself. Aresmode never writes a value it estimated or inferred. Recovery and mobility sessions are not written at all. If you delete a session in Aresmode, the workout Aresmode wrote for it is deleted from Apple Health as well; Aresmode can only delete samples it wrote itself, and never touches data written by your watch or by other apps. Write permission is asked separately from read permission and declining it changes nothing else in the app. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

Automatically Collected

  • Pseudonymous usage data: a hashed random installation identifier, fixed product-interaction events, and low-cardinality properties such as mode, discipline, and feature source
  • Limited diagnostics: a capped technical exception name or error context; no question, journal, workout content, health value, name, or email is included
  • Feedback you choose to send, together with app version/build, generic device model, OS version, locale, app language, and send time

Device Permissions

Aresmode requests the following permissions. Each is optional and requested only when you use the related feature:

  • Microphone — voice workout logging
  • Speech Recognition — transcribing voice logs through Apple Speech on iOS or the configured Android speech-recognition service
  • Camera (iOS) — taking an optional background photo for a share card
  • System photo picker / Photo Library — selecting media for analysis or saving share cards and reports you generate
  • Apple Health or Health Connect — reading workout and recovery data, and on iOS writing back the workouts you complete in the app (see above)
  • Notifications — optional workout, motivation, check-in, and weigh-in reminders

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver AI-powered workout prescriptions and analysis
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • To send administrative information (policy updates, etc.)
  • To protect our services and prevent fraud
  • To evaluate and improve our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only with the following third parties:

  • Rork Toolkit / Vercel AI Gateway — infrastructure that routes AI requests to the selected model provider
  • OpenAI — AI models that generate workout prescriptions and read text, voice-log transcripts, submitted photos, and selected pages from a weekly-plan document
  • Google — Google Sign-In, Google Play billing, Health Connect on Android, and Gemini models that analyze still keyframes extracted on your device from the training, match, or posture video you select; the complete video is not uploaded
  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management
  • TelemetryDeck — pseudonymous product-interaction and limited diagnostic events
  • Formspree — feedback you explicitly submit and the attached technical context listed above
  • Apple — iOS account authentication, payment processing, Apple Health, and Speech Recognition. Live voice audio may be sent to Apple's Speech service; Aresmode does not retain the raw audio.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Aresmode contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.

4. AI-Based Services

Aresmode uses AI models from two providers, and which one receives your data depends on what you submit:

  • OpenAI — personalized workout prescriptions, coaching answers, and interpretation of text, voice-log transcripts, up to five selected photos, or the first five pages of a weekly-plan PDF. Training context such as age, gender, body metrics, logged sessions, goals, selected condition values, and self-reported pain may be sent for processing.
  • Google (Gemini) — analysis of still keyframes extracted on your device from a training, match, or posture video you explicitly select. The complete source video is not uploaded.

Requests to both providers are routed through our infrastructure provider (Rork Toolkit / Vercel AI Gateway). Media is sent only when you explicitly submit it for analysis.

AI output is for training guidance only and is not medical advice. Aresmode does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe for any medical condition.

5. Data Retention

Training records and analysis results are stored locally on your device until you delete them, permanently delete the account, or remove the app. Posture cleanup targets the five most recent completed or replay clips; a clip for an interrupted analysis can be kept temporarily so the analysis can resume. Measurements and reports can remain after an older replay clip is pruned. Match and training review videos use temporary local copies, while only selected still keyframes are sent for requested AI commentary.

External processors retain or delete data under their service settings, contracts, and applicable legal obligations. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain transaction records required for accounting, fraud prevention, or law. When you permanently delete an account, Aresmode removes that account's local store and media after server confirmation; this does not erase Apple Health or Health Connect source records, or transaction records controlled by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.

Feedback messages are retained by Formspree and the recipient email account until removed under their service settings and deletion procedures.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
